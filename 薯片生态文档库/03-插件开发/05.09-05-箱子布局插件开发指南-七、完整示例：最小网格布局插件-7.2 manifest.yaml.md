### 7.2 `manifest.yaml`

```yaml
id: chips.layout.grid
version: 1.0.0
type: boxlayout
name: 网格布局
description: 以网格方式排列箱子条目
author: 薯片官方
license: MIT
runtime:
  targets: [host]
permissions:
  - resources:read
capabilities:
  layouts:
    - id: chips.layout.grid
      name: 网格布局
      icon: assets/icons/grid.svg
      description: 自适应网格排列条目
      configSchema: assets/schemas/grid-config.schema.json
      schemaVersions: ">=1.0.0 <2.0.0"
```
